CropGuardian was engineered to protect smallholder farmers, agronomists, and commercial growers from catastrophic yield loss caused by plant diseases. Why It Was Made

Traditional agricultural pathology requires laboratory culture tests taking 5–10 days. By the time symptoms are confirmed, fungal mycelium and bacterial blights have already spread across entire farm acres. CropGuardian solves this with instant vision AI.
